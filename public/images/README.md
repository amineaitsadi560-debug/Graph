# Real photos of the gym

The site shows original vector illustrations (labelled "Illustration") until you add real photos.
Drop JPG files here with these exact names and they replace the illustrations automatically:

- `about.jpg` — "La salle" section
- `strength.jpg` — musculation section
- `cardio.jpg` — cardio section

Only use photos of your own gym that you have the right to publish (landscape, ~1600×1200, under 400 KB).
