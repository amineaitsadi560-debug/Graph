# Images

These files are used by the site. Replace any of them with your own photos of the gym (same name, JPG, ~1200-1900 px wide).

- `hero.jpg` — hero background (wide). The dark left half is where the headline sits.
- `hero-rtl.jpg` — the same photo mirrored, used for Arabic (right-to-left) so the text stays clear. Make it by flipping hero.jpg horizontally.
- `strength.jpg`, `cardio.jpg`, `about.jpg` — section pictures (portrait/landscape both work).

The current files are AI-generated images (they are labelled "Image générée par IA" on the site). Remove that label by replacing them with real photos and deleting the `photo_ai` captions in `src/components/Art.tsx` and `src/site/Home.tsx`.
Logos and slogans were removed from them on purpose: do not use images that show other brands' logos.
